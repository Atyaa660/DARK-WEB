DARK WEB

Confidential Project Vision & Development Brief

«A fictional interactive world where every choice can change what comes next.»

Version: 0.4 Prototype
Status: Early Development
Current Technology: Python
Current Platform: Terminal
Target Platform: Mobile
Languages: English / Arabic

---

1. The Vision

DARK WEB is not intended to be just another text-based game.

The current prototype is the foundation of a larger interactive experience built around one central idea:

«Give the player a world to explore, decisions to make, and consequences to discover.»

The player enters an unfamiliar fictional world with limited resources and very little knowledge.

From that point forward, the world gradually reveals itself.

People have their own motives.

Opportunities appear and disappear.

Reputation matters.

Resources matter.

Decisions matter.

And the player's previous actions can influence what happens next.

---

2. The Core Experience

The intended gameplay loop is built around:

Explore
   ↓
Discover
   ↓
Interact
   ↓
Choose
   ↓
Gain / Lose
   ↓
Build Reputation
   ↓
Unlock New Possibilities
   ↓
Change the Story

The objective is to make progression feel like the result of the player's actions rather than simply increasing a level number.

---

3. The World

The prototype currently contains the foundations of a fictional city.

Current locations include:

- Harbor
- Market
- Cafe
- Old Warehouse

These locations are intentionally simple at this stage.

They are the starting framework for a much larger world containing:

- Characters
- Missions
- Events
- Discoveries
- Relationships
- Resources
- Story progression

The final goal is for locations to feel like places within a living world, rather than simple menu options.

---

4. The Player

The player currently has several core attributes:

- Name
- Level
- Money
- Reputation
- Location
- Inventory

These systems form the foundation for future gameplay.

The player should gradually become more than a collection of statistics.

Their actions, relationships, discoveries, and decisions should contribute to their identity within the world.

---

5. Reputation

Reputation is one of the important pillars of the project.

The player's reputation may eventually influence:

- Character behavior
- Available opportunities
- Mission availability
- Prices
- Events
- Story progression

The purpose is simple:

The world should remember what the player has done.

---

6. Economy

Money is currently a basic gameplay resource.

The long-term goal is to turn the economy into a meaningful part of the experience.

The player may eventually use resources to:

- Purchase items
- Access opportunities
- Progress through certain situations
- Make strategic decisions
- Influence outcomes

The economy should interact with the rest of the game rather than exist as an isolated number.

---

7. Missions

The prototype already contains a basic mission system.

Current examples include:

- Explore the Harbor
- Visit the Market
- Talk to the Mysterious Man

The final mission system is intended to support:

- Objectives
- Requirements
- Rewards
- Multiple stages
- Dependencies
- Consequences
- Story integration

A major technical goal is to create a reusable mission framework so new content can be added without rewriting the game engine.

---

8. Characters

Characters are intended to become an important part of the world.

The prototype currently contains basic character interaction.

Future characters may have:

- Personal goals
- Relationships
- Different attitudes
- Changing dialogue
- Hidden information
- Individual story relevance

A character should not simply exist to display text.

A good character should create possibilities and consequences.

---

9. Dialogue

Dialogue will eventually become more than a sequence of messages.

The long-term system should support:

- Player choices
- Different responses
- Relationship changes
- Reputation effects
- Story consequences
- Conditional dialogue

The objective is to make conversations part of gameplay.

---

10. Dynamic Events

One of the major future systems is a dynamic event layer.

Events may depend on conditions such as:

- Player progression
- Reputation
- Money
- Location
- Previous missions
- Inventory
- Previous decisions

This creates the possibility of a world that reacts differently to different players.

---

11. Branching Progression

The project is designed with branching progression in mind.

A player's decision should be capable of influencing future possibilities.

For example, a decision could potentially:

- Open a new opportunity.
- Close another opportunity.
- Change a relationship.
- Alter a future event.
- Affect reputation.
- Change the player's available path.

The exact narrative structure and unreleased mechanics are intentionally not documented publicly.

---

12. Architecture Direction

The current prototype is intentionally compact because the priority has been validating the gameplay concept.

As development progresses, the project should evolve toward a modular architecture.

Potential systems include:

Player
World
Locations
Missions
Characters
Dialogue
Inventory
Economy
Events
Story
Save System
Localization

The exact architecture should be determined collaboratively with an experienced developer.

The prototype should not become a limitation.

---

13. Content-Driven Design

A major development objective is to separate game systems from game content.

Ideally, adding a new:

- Character
- Mission
- Location
- Item
- Dialogue
- Event

should not require rewriting unrelated systems.

This will allow the project to grow significantly without turning the codebase into an unmanageable collection of special cases.

---

14. Localization

DARK WEB is intended to support:

English

and

Arabic

as first-class languages.

Localization should eventually cover:

- Interface
- Story
- Missions
- Dialogue
- Items
- Locations
- System messages

Arabic support is particularly important because the project is intended to be accessible to Arabic-speaking players without sacrificing the international audience.

---

15. Mobile Vision

The current Terminal version is a prototype.

The long-term goal is a mobile game with a proper visual interface.

The mobile version should eventually include:

- Touch-friendly navigation
- Visual menus
- Dialogue screens
- Inventory interface
- Character interface
- World navigation
- Story presentation
- Save / Load
- Arabic / English support
- Audio
- Visual assets

The Terminal prototype exists to prove the underlying concept before investing heavily in presentation.

---
